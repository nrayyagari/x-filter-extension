X Filter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Overview

X Filter is a Chrome extension that filters unwanted content from X.com (Twitter) timelines. This privacy policy explains how the extension handles data.

Data Collection

X Filter does not collect any personal data.

  • No personally identifiable information is collected
  • No browsing history is collected
  • No analytics or telemetry data is sent
  • No data is transmitted to any external server

Data Storage

All extension data is stored locally on your device using chrome.storage.local:

  • Filter preferences (which categories are active)
  • Keyword lists (political, movies, sensationalism, tech protection)
  • Learned patterns (authors and hashtags you've marked as "Hide similar")
  • Whitelisted authors
  • Usage statistics (number of posts hidden, patterns learned)

This data never leaves your device. It is not synced, not uploaded, and not shared.

Permissions

  • storage — Used to save your filter settings and learned patterns locally on your device.
  • activeTab — Used to interact with the X.com tab when you click the extension icon.
  • host_permissions (x.com, twitter.com) — Required for the content script to scan and filter tweets on your timeline. No data is read from or sent to any other website.

Third-Party Services

X Filter does not use any third-party services, APIs, analytics, advertising networks, or tracking scripts.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored locally and persists until you:

  • Click "Reset All Learned Data" in the extension settings, or
  • Uninstall the extension (which removes all stored data), or
  • Clear your browser's local storage

Children's Privacy

X Filter does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
